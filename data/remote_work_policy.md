NORTHBRIDGE ANALYTICS
REMOTE WORK, EQUIPMENT AND EXPENSE POLICY
Effective March 1, 2026. Policy owner: People Operations.

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This policy explains who may work remotely, what the company provides for a home office, and how work-related expenses are approved and repaid. It applies to all full-time and part-time employees. Contractors and interns follow the terms in their own agreements, except that Section 9 (Data Security) applies to everyone who holds a company laptop. Where this policy conflicts with a local law, the local law applies.

2. ELIGIBILITY FOR REMOTE WORK
An employee becomes eligible to request a remote arrangement after completing a probationary period of 90 days. Roles that require daily access to the hardware lab or the mail room are not eligible. Requests are made through the HR portal and need approval from both the employee's manager and People Operations. A decision is given within 10 business days of the request.

3. TYPES OF ARRANGEMENT
Hybrid employees work from the office at least two days per week, chosen with their team. Fully remote employees may work from home every day, but must attend the quarterly planning week in person at the Boston headquarters. Fully remote status is limited to employees who live more than 50 miles from any company office. An employee may switch between hybrid and fully remote status once every 12 months.

4. CORE HOURS AND AVAILABILITY
All employees, remote or not, must be available between 11:00 a.m. and 3:00 p.m. Eastern Time on working days. Outside core hours, employees may arrange their schedule with their manager. Employees must reply to messages from their own team within four working hours during core hours. Remote employees keep their camera on for team meetings unless their connection cannot support video.

5. HOME OFFICE STIPEND
Each remote or hybrid employee receives a one-time home office stipend of $600 in their first remote year, for items such as a desk, chair or monitor. After the first year, a yearly refresh allowance of $200 is available for replacing worn equipment. Stipend purchases must be made within 120 days of approval, and unused stipend money does not roll over. Items bought with the stipend belong to the employee.

6. INTERNET AND PHONE
The company reimburses up to $50 per month toward home internet for fully remote employees and up to $25 per month for hybrid employees. Mobile phone costs are reimbursed only for employees in an on-call rotation, up to $40 per month. These amounts are claimed through the monthly expense report, not as a stipend.

7. COMPANY EQUIPMENT
Every employee receives a company laptop. Remote employees may also borrow one external monitor and a docking station from the IT equipment pool. Company equipment remains company property and must not be used by family members or other people. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours. When employment ends, all company equipment must be returned within 14 days, using the prepaid shipping label IT provides.

8. EXPENSE SUBMISSION AND REIMBURSEMENT
Work-related expenses are submitted in the expense tool with an itemized receipt for every purchase over $25. Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date; claims older than 60 days are not reimbursed. Expenses up to $500 need the manager's approval; anything above $500 also needs approval from the Finance team. Approved expenses are paid with the next payroll run, which happens on the 15th and the last business day of each month.

9. DATA SECURITY
Company work may only be done on company devices. Laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after five minutes of inactivity. When working outside the home, for example in a cafe or a shared workspace, employees must connect through the company VPN and must not use public printers. Customer data may never be stored on personal cloud accounts, USB drives or personal email.

10. TRAVEL TO THE OFFICE
Fully remote employees who travel for the quarterly planning week are reimbursed for economy airfare or train fare, hotel up to $220 per night, and meals up to $75 per day. Hybrid employees' daily commute is not reimbursed. Travel must be booked through the company travel portal at least 14 days in advance unless the trip was scheduled with less notice.

11. WORKING FROM ANOTHER COUNTRY
Employees may work from outside their home country for up to 20 working days in a calendar year, with their manager's approval requested at least 30 days before the trip. Longer stays need written approval from People Operations and Legal, because of tax and immigration rules. Working from a country under export restrictions is never allowed.

12. ENDING OR CHANGING AN ARRANGEMENT
The company may end or change a remote arrangement for business reasons with 30 days' written notice. An employee who wants to return to the office full time gives their manager 14 days' notice. If a remote arrangement ends because of repeated performance problems, notice is shortened to 7 days.

13. QUESTIONS AND EXCEPTIONS
Questions about this policy go to People Operations at people-ops@northbridge.example. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the Head of People Operations and are reviewed every six months.
